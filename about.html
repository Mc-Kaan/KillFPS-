About KillFPS+

A lightweight Fabric performance modpack focused on higher FPS and smoother gameplay.

What is it?

KillFPS+ is a curated collection of optimization mods for Minecraft Java Edition (Fabric). The goal is simple: give you more frames and less lag without bloating the game with unnecessary content.

Supported Versions

  • Minecraft 1.21.11
  • Minecraft 1.21.4
  • Minecraft 1.21.1

Philosophy

Only mods that clearly improve performance or provide essential quality-of-life features are included. No heavy content mods, no unnecessary libraries. Keep it fast and clean.
